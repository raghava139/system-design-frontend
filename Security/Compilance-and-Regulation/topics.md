# Compliance & Regulations — Interview Notes

## 1. Simple Definition

> **Regulation = Rules / laws that the organization must follow.**
>
> **Compliance = Making sure the application and organization follow those rules.**

---

## 2. What Does Compliance Mean for a Developer?

As a developer, compliance means understanding the applicable requirements and implementing the necessary **technical controls** in the application.
# Compliance & Regulations — Interview Notes

**1. Data Privacy →** Protect personal and sensitive user/customer data.
**2. Access Control →** Ensure only authorized users can access specific data and features.
**3. Authentication →** Verify the identity of users securely.
**4. Authorization / RBAC →** Give users only the permissions required for their role.
**5. Audit Logging →** Record important user actions, changes, timestamps, and relevant details for traceability.
**6. Data Retention →** Keep data only for the required legal, regulatory, and business period.
**7. Data Security →** Protect sensitive data using encryption, secure APIs, access controls, and secure storage.
**8. Data Integrity →** Ensure important data remains accurate, consistent, and protected from unauthorized modification.
**9. Data Residency →** Follow applicable requirements regarding where data is stored and processed.
**10. Third-Party Compliance →** Ensure external vendors and services follow applicable security and compliance requirements.
**11. Backup & Recovery →** Maintain secure backups and recovery processes for critical data.
**12. Monitoring & Incident Management →** Detect, record, investigate, and respond to security or compliance incidents.
**13. Industry Regulations →** Follow additional requirements based on the industry, such as finance, healthcare, or logistics.
**14. Country / Regional Regulations →** Identify and follow regulations applicable to the countries or regions where the application operates.
**15. Security Audits →** Regularly review, test, and verify that security and compliance requirements are being followed.

## Interview Memory

**Regulation →** Rule / law that must be followed.

**Compliance →** Following and demonstrating adherence to those rules.

**Developer →** Convert applicable compliance requirements into technical controls.

**Important →** Applicable regulations depend on the **country, industry, data, business, and application**.
